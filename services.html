Five ways to work with us, from a first assessment to full system optimisation.

The five services

Every building is different, so we don't sell one fixed package. These are the five ways clients typically engage us, individually or in sequence, depending on whether you're starting from nothing, refitting an existing building, or already running a system you want to improve.

  1. 01

    Autonomy assessment

    Find out how close your building can realistically get to full energy autonomy before you commit to anything.

    We assess an existing or proposed building against your energy, resilience and cost objectives, and give you a clear, evidenced answer: what full or partial autonomy would look like for this specific site, and what it would take to get there.

    Best for: building owners, developers or local authorities who want an independent answer before committing budget to a design.

    Request an assessment
  2. 02

    Autonomous building design

    Integrated system design, building modelling and energy architecture, with the envelope, generation, storage, heat, mobility and controls designed as one system.

    We design the full energy architecture for a new or retrofitted building, so the envelope, renewable generation, storage, thermal systems, EV charging and controls work together, rather than as separate specs handed to different contractors.

    Best for: projects moving from concept to a buildable design, where you want one coherent system rather than five separate vendor specs.

    Discuss a design brief
  3. 03

    Digital energy modelling

    We design and develop an autonomous building in a virtual environment and test the system in simulation before a single component is specified.

    Using EnergyPlus-based modelling, we simulate building demand, PV generation, battery storage, thermal performance, EV charging and control strategies under your actual climate and occupancy conditions, so sizing decisions are based on evidence, not rules of thumb.

    Best for: teams who need to validate a design, compare technology options, or support a funding or planning case with defensible figures.

    Ask about modelling for your project
  4. 04

    Research and publications

    We conduct research with the aim of publishing in leading peer-reviewed journals.

    This can be research into a specific energy-autonomous building system you already have in mind. Or we can design and develop the autonomous building for you in a virtual environment, running energy simulations, monitoring results and collecting data for your selected climate location.

    Once the autonomous system is operational, we optimise how generation, storage, demand and mobility interact in practice, closing the gap between the design model and how the autonomous building actually behaves.

    Best for: buildings running on 100% clean heat and energy, or with an energy storage system that is not performing to spec or has never been formally implemented.

    Talk about an existing system
  5. 05

    Technology & methodology

    The modelling frameworks and system architecture behind our own projects, available to partners building at scale.

    For organisations planning multiple autonomous-building projects (housing associations, developers, local or regional government programmes), we make our underlying methodology and system architecture available as a partnership, rather than running every project as a one-off.

    Best for: partners planning a programme of buildings rather than a single site.

    Discuss a partnership

Not sure where to start?

  • Nothing built yet, or considering a retrofit? Start with an assessment.
  • Ready to move from concept to a buildable design? Go straight to design.
  • Need evidence to support a funding, planning or investment case? Start with modelling.
