The Concept 5 Zero

The framework and system architecture behind every autonomous building we design.

Our approach

One framework, five outcomes

We introduce the novel concept of 5Zero autonomous buildings: buildings characterised by zero carbon, zero energy, zero grid connection, zero energy bills and zero-emission mobility.

An autonomous building (AB) built on this concept provides a full year of net-zero carbon emissions and zero energy bills for both the home and the electric vehicle (EV), an approach unprecedented globally.

The Concept 5 Zero was introduced by our founder, Saeed Mohammadi, and originates from his research into energy-autonomous buildings at the University of Strathclyde.

The 5 Zero framework

  • 01

    Zero energy

    The building generates at least as much energy as it uses.

  • 02

    Zero carbon

    Clean generation, storage and heat remove operational carbon emissions.

  • 03

    Zero grid connection

    The building can run without dependence on outside energy networks.

  • 04

    Zero energy bills

    Owning production, storage and management of energy removes recurring energy costs, and where possible surplus energy can be sold back.

  • 05

    Zero-emission mobility

    EV charging is powered by the building's own clean energy.

AB + EV + 5Z

The building and the vehicle, together

A "greened" electric autonomous building and electric vehicle does more than power one home.

It creates reasonable assumptions for more EV charging points in remote villages, rural and coastal areas, highlands and islands, suburban towns and off-grid locations worldwide. It also creates reasonable assumptions for EV efficiency and for the carbon rates of the electricity sector.

Home battery green electric vehicles (HBGEVs) offer a novel path to reducing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from both the transport and building sectors.

Bringing the AB, EV and 5Zero package into green transport and building practice can lead to substantial advances in sustainability.

The system architecture

We design the whole chain as one system. Every component is sized and controlled against every other, using simulation-based building modelling before anything is specified or installed.

  1. 01

    Envelope

    The building fabric, designed to reduce demand before anything is generated.

  2. 02

    Generation

    On-site renewable generation, such as PV, sized against the building's real demand.

  3. 03

    Storage

    Battery storage sized against both generation and demand.

  4. 04

    Thermal

    Clean heat and thermal systems integrated with generation and storage.

  5. 05

    EV charging

    Mobility brought inside the building's energy balance.

  6. 06

    Intelligent controls

    The controls that tie generation, storage, heat and mobility together.

  7. 07

    Autonomy

    A building that produces, stores and manages its own energy.

Tested in simulation first

Every design decision is tested in simulation before it's specified, using the same EnergyPlus-based modelling as our own research, so performance claims are backed by a model you can interrogate, not a brochure figure.

Our founder is the lead developer of the Energy Custom Sizing Toolkit (ECST), a purpose-built tool for sizing PV arrays and battery storage for fully off-grid buildings.
