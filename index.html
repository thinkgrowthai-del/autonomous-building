Buildings that generate, store and manage their own energy, on-grid or fully off-grid.

We design and engineer the whole system: envelope, generation, storage, clean heat and energy, EV charging, and the controls that tie it together. The result is a building that can run without dependence on outside energy networks.

Peer-reviewed research foundation (University of Strathclyde) · Simulation-led engineering · Scotland to the Persian Gulf

Talk to the team

A PV company sells panels. A battery company sells storage. A heating contractor sells a heat pump.

Each solves one piece and leaves you to make them work together.

We design the whole chain as one system:

  1. 01

    Envelope

    The building fabric, designed to reduce demand before anything is generated.

  2. 02

    Generation

    On-site renewable generation, such as PV, sized against the building's real demand.

  3. 03

    Storage

    Battery storage sized against both generation and demand.

  4. 04

    Thermal

    Clean heat and thermal systems integrated with generation and storage.

  5. 05

    EV charging

    Mobility brought inside the building's energy balance.

  6. 06

    Intelligent controls

    The controls that tie generation, storage, heat and mobility together.

  7. The outcome

    Autonomy

    A building that produces, stores and manages its own energy.

Every component is sized and controlled against every other, using simulation-based building modelling before anything is specified or installed.

The 5 Zero framework

Zero

Explore The Concept 5 Zero
  1. 01

    Zero energy

    The building generates at least as much energy as it uses.

  2. 02

    Zero carbon

    Clean generation, storage and heat remove operational carbon emissions.

  3. 03

    Zero grid connection

    The building can run without dependence on outside energy networks.

  4. 04

    Zero energy bills

    Owning production, storage and management of energy removes recurring energy costs.

  5. 05

    Zero-emission mobility

    EV charging is powered by the building's own clean energy.
