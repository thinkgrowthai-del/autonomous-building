Where we work

Scotland, the UK, Europe, USA, UAE and the Persian Gulf, across building types from schools to off-grid housing.

Regions

Autonomous design has to work with local climate, energy prices and grid conditions. We model each building under its actual climate and occupancy before anything is specified.

  • Scotland

    Where our research foundation began, at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow.

  • United Kingdom

    New and retrofitted buildings moving toward full or partial energy autonomy.

  • Europe

    Integrated energy systems for buildings across a wide range of climates.

  • USA

    Energy-autonomous design for new developments and existing buildings.

  • UAE

    Generation, storage and thermal systems designed for high-demand, hot-climate buildings.

  • Persian Gulf

    Off-grid energy modelling for buildings in demanding climates, a focus of our founder's research.

Sites where the grid is weakest

We work across rural and coastal areas, desert and remote villages, highlands and islands, off-grid locations worldwide and individual sites as readily as urban ones, because autonomous design has to hold up where the grid is weakest or doesn't exist, not just where it's strongest.

Key points

Energy autonomy, built around place and community

  1. 01

    Place-based approaches help autonomous communities and individuals own their energy systems, take direct control and navigate the net zero transition, supporting thriving local housing, industries and communities.

  2. 02

    Energy-autonomous buildings foster connections between the built environment, energy, communities, transport, industry and government, supporting vibrant regional communities through the energy security and net zero transition.

  3. 03

    Communities are key to co-designing sustainable autonomous energy solutions that align with local needs and values.
