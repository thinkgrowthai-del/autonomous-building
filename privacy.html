Privacy notice

How we use the information you send us through this website.

Who we are

Autonomous Building Technology ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal information you send us through this website. You can contact us about this notice at info@autonomous-building.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to enter in our contact form: the comment type, your name, company, email address and phone number, the service you're interested in, and your comments. We don't ask for anything else.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and, if you ask us to, to discuss a project with you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps you have asked for before entering into a contract.

Who handles it

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, a form-handling service, which may process data outside the UK. We don't sell your details or share them with anyone else for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any project that follows.

Cookies and third-party content

This website doesn't set cookies or use analytics. Our fonts load from Google Fonts and our photographs from Unsplash, so those services receive your device's IP address when a page loads.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email info@autonomous-building.co.uk and we'll respond. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
