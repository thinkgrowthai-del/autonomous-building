About Autonomous Building Technology

Founder & team

Mission

Own your energy, not just your equipment.

Most energy-transition advice still means buying a slightly more efficient boiler, a slightly better panel, a slightly bigger battery: one purchase at a time, from one vendor at a time. We think that's the wrong unit of design.

Autonomous Building Technology exists to engineer a building's envelope, generation, storage, heat, mobility and control as one integrated design, so the building itself produces, stores and manages its own energy rather than depending on external supply.

We don't want you to keep buying energy from a network. We want you to own the production, storage and management of it and, where possible, to sell it back.

Founder

Saeed Mohammadi

Founder, CEO & Chief Scientist

Saeed founded Autonomous Building Technology to bring his research on energy-autonomous buildings out of the lab and into practice. His research into energy self-sufficient buildings was carried out in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Strathclyde, Glasgow, covering autonomous building design, clean energy systems and off-grid energy modelling for buildings in demanding climates, including the Persian Gulf region.

He is the lead developer of the Energy Custom Sizing Toolkit (ECST), a purpose-built tool for sizing PV arrays and battery storage for fully off-grid buildings, and uses EnergyPlus simulation modelling throughout his research. Saeed introduced “The Concept 5 Zero”, the framework behind our approach, which grew out of that work.

How we work

Every design decision is tested in simulation before it's specified, using the same EnergyPlus-based modelling as our own research, so performance claims are backed by a model you can interrogate, not a brochure figure.

We work across rural, coastal, island and off-grid sites as readily as urban ones, because autonomous design has to hold up where the grid is weakest or doesn't exist, not just where it's strongest.
