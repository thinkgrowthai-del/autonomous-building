Our research

A peer-reviewed research foundation, built at the University of Strathclyde, Glasgow.

Research foundation

From the lab into practice

Autonomous Building Technology was founded to bring research on energy-autonomous buildings out of the lab and into practice.

That research was carried out in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Strathclyde. It covers autonomous building design, clean energy systems and off-grid energy modelling for buildings in demanding climates, including the Persian Gulf region.

  • Autonomy
  • Building modelling
  • Carbon reduction to zero
  • Decentralisation of energy
  • Energy independence

Research areas

  • 01

    Autonomous building design

    Buildings that produce, store and manage their own energy as one integrated system.

  • 02

    Clean energy systems

    Generation, storage, clean heat and EV charging designed to work together.

  • 03

    Off-grid energy modelling

    Modelling buildings in demanding climates, including the Persian Gulf region.

  • 04

    EnergyPlus simulation

    Simulation-based building modelling used throughout our research and design work.

  • 05

    PV and battery sizing

    The Energy Custom Sizing Toolkit (ECST), a purpose-built tool for sizing PV arrays and battery storage for fully off-grid buildings.

  • 06

    The Concept 5 Zero

    The framework behind our approach, which originates from this research. Read about the approach.
